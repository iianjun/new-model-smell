# Repository guide

## Work map

- `CONTEXT.md` owns product terminology and its avoided alternatives.
- Product requirements, task status, and acceptance criteria live under `docs/issues/`; product behavior changes start from the relevant issue.
- Long-lived interaction and visual intent live under `docs/design/`; architecture decisions and their rationale live under `docs/adr/`.
- Repository contribution conventions are indexed in `docs/conventions/index.md`.

## Verification

- `pnpm validate` is the repository-wide definition of done; `README.md` documents the narrower quality commands.
