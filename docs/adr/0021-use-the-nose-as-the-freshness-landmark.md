# Use The Nose as the freshness landmark

Motor Town's central landmark will be The Nose: a giant painted enamel nose presented as a premium automotive sculpture on a matte mechanical turntable. It faces the Dealership whose Flagship Lineup contains the newest Flagship Launch, inhales visible pixel scent particles during the opening reveal, and reports `NEW MODEL SMELL REMAINING` on a physical gauge. Once driving begins, it holds that freshness orientation instead of tracking or reacting to nearby vehicles.

The Nose makes current release recency visible without reducing it to dashboard text, provides a stable navigation landmark, and connects the title directly to the physical world. Its premium presentation remains serious while the opening sneeze supplies the joke without distracting from driving.
