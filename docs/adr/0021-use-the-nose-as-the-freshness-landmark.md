# Use The Nose as the freshness landmark

Motor Town's central landmark will be The Nose: a giant painted enamel nose presented as a premium automotive sculpture on a matte mechanical turntable. It rotates toward the Dealership whose Flagship Lineup contains the newest Flagship Launch, inhales visible pixel scent particles, and reports `NEW MODEL SMELL REMAINING` on a physical gauge. It reacts to nearby vehicles by tracking them and occasionally producing an exaggerated sneeze.

The Nose makes current release recency visible without reducing it to dashboard text, provides a navigation landmark, and connects the title directly to the physical world. Its premium presentation remains serious while its literal behavior supplies the joke.
