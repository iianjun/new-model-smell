# Use forgiving arcade driving physics

Motor Town will use forgiving arcade vehicle physics rather than realistic driving simulation. The Inspector Cart is slow, tightly steering, and visibly wobbly. The Model Vehicle is faster and heavier, has a broader turning radius, and permits a small controlled drift. Collisions cause playful bounce without damage or failure. The physical chassis locks pitch and roll so a vehicle cannot overturn; a vehicle that falls out of the world or becomes irretrievably trapped returns automatically to controllable driving.

Both vehicles share `WASD` and arrow-key acceleration, reverse, and steering, with automatic direction changes and `Space` as a short handbrake. Vehicle character comes from tuning and presentation rather than separate control schemes. Driving must remain expressive enough to enjoy but must not prevent a visitor from reaching the site's information.
