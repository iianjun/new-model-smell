# Reveal Motor Town with The Nose's sneeze

The opening will begin in an extreme close view of The Nose inhaling pixel scent particles until `FRESHNESS EVENT DETECTED` illuminates. Its exaggerated sneeze pushes the camera up and back to reveal Motor Town, blows the cover off the Inspector Cart, and hands control to the visitor with `WASD — BEGIN INSPECTION` painted onto the road.

The reveal will be performed in real time with the same Three.js world and objects used during play, not as a rendered video. It must be short, skippable with any key, and replaced with a calm abbreviated reveal when reduced motion is requested. This concentrates the initial spectacle in a reusable world object and transitions directly into control without a loading-screen-style cinematic cut.
