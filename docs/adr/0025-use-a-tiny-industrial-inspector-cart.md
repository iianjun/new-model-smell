# Use a tiny industrial Inspector Cart

The Inspector Cart will combine the compact proportions of an airport baggage tug and a golf cart. It uses a short, tall body, deliberately small wheels, warm-ivory and safety-orange paint, a roof-mounted rotating smell detector, a loosely mounted inspection clipboard, and an overly serious industrial reverse alarm.

Its small upright silhouette must contrast clearly with the broad, low Model Vehicle from the high chase camera. It will also be visibly small enough to fit inside the Model Vehicle's exaggerated trunk during a Valet Transfer, supporting the physical joke without requiring an unexplained disappearance.
