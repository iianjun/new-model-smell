# Keep Showrooms seamless with cutaway buildings

Dealership Showrooms will remain physically connected to Motor Town instead of loading a separate interior scene. A visitor drives the Inspector Cart through the Dealership entrance while the same high three-quarter chase camera continues to follow it. As the vehicle approaches or enters, the roof and camera-facing walls fade or disappear to reveal a miniature cutaway interior.

This preserves the continuous driving fantasy and avoids a camera change while keeping the Showroom readable from the chosen elevated viewpoint. The building must therefore be designed around clear vehicle paths, low interior partitions, generous entrances, and replaceable roof and wall sections rather than as a realistic enclosed building.
