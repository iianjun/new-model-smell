# Use a bright painted inspection-town palette

Motor Town will use a bright daytime palette built from warm ivory architecture, charcoal asphalt, safety-orange interaction accents, faded green landscaping, and a pale blue sky. Tracked Company colors remain limited to Company Trims, Dealership identification, and other small wayfinding details so they do not fragment the shared world.

Machinery and landmarks will not use chrome as a decorative material. The Nose, Dyno Lab, Valet Transfer Bays, and other equipment instead use matte painted metal, enamel panels, dark rubber, and restrained non-reflective hardware. This keeps the low-poly pixel presentation stable and tactile while avoiding a glossy luxury-showroom appearance that would fight the playful late-1990s arcade character.
