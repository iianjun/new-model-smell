# Pull the Dyno Sheet into the Dossier

After a Dyno run, the machine will print a physical paper strip that the visitor grabs and pulls with the pointer. The strip unfurls from the three-dimensional world toward the camera and resolves into the crisp two-dimensional Model Dossier. Closing the dossier folds and retracts the sheet into the machine before returning control to the same Active Flagship and location.

This transition gives the pointer a deliberate object interaction, connects the physical Dyno ritual to readable benchmark information, and avoids an abrupt modal appearing solely from a proximity trigger. Equivalent pointer and pull behavior must remain possible for mouse, trackpad, and touch input.
