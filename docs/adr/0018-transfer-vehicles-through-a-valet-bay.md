# Transfer vehicles through a Valet Bay

Visitors will board a Flagship Model Vehicle by parking the Inspector Cart in a marked Valet Transfer Bay behind that vehicle. A deliberately over-engineered luxury handover mechanism aligns the Cart, packs it into the Flagship's trunk, wakes the Flagship, and transfers control without opening a selection menu or changing cameras. The visitor then performs the Drive-Out manually.

This keeps vehicle choice inside the physical world, clearly associates the choice with a specific displayed Flagship, and turns an otherwise abstract model-selection action into a memorable object interaction. The machinery should be staged with premium seriousness while the literal storage of the Inspector Cart provides the joke.
