# Shape the Model Vehicle as a 1990s concept grand tourer

The shared Model Vehicle will use the proportions of a broad, angular 1990s concept grand tourer. Its low wedge-shaped nose, large glass canopy, planar shoulders, and oversized wheels must remain clearly readable from the high three-quarter chase camera. The design should communicate flagship prestige and speed without reproducing a recognizable production vehicle or marque.

All Company Trims retain the same base silhouette and driving footprint. They differentiate Flagship Models through controlled colors, light signatures, badges, and a small set of replaceable body panels. This keeps a single ownable hero asset while allowing a Flagship Lineup to display multiple distinguishable instances.
