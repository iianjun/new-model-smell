# Render low-poly 3D with a clean pixel-art vocabulary

Motor Town, its vehicles, and physical effects will be built as low-poly three-dimensional objects with a deliberately color-controlled pixel-art vocabulary. The style will resemble a bright, stable late-1990s arcade or N64-era world through its forms, palette, materials, and restrained detail rather than through low output resolution, jagged raster edges, PS1-style geometry jitter, or dark neon cyberpunk.

The default renderer should produce a clean image at the display's native density, capped at DPR 2, with antialiased edges. Lower render density may be offered later as an explicit performance setting, but it is not part of the visual identity. This preserves free driving, physics, dynamic lighting, and object interaction while creating a playful identity distinct from a smooth low-poly portfolio. Model Dossiers remain crisp two-dimensional interfaces so benchmarks and articles stay readable.
