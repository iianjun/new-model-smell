# Place model content at shared town destinations

Visitors will continue driving an Active Flagship through Motor Town after leaving its Dealership, then take that vehicle to shared Content Destinations: the Dyno Lab for benchmark evidence and the Drive-In for articles. A destination opens a readable two-dimensional Model Dossier while preserving the vehicle state, and closing it resumes driving in the same vehicle and location. This keeps content legible without reducing discovery to a conventional menu.
