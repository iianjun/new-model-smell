# 06 — Connect The Nose to Model Freshness

**What to build:** Turn The Nose from an opening prop into Motor Town's live Model Freshness landmark. It must orient toward the Dealership containing the newest represented Flagship Launch, communicate freshness without implying model quality, and react physically when a visitor drives nearby.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Enter Motor Town through The Nose's sneeze; 04 — Visit the OpenAI cutaway Showroom.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Nose is a literal painted-enamel nose on a matte mechanical turntable, with no decorative chrome.
- [ ] Its orientation is derived from the newest Flagship Launch in the loaded Tracked Company data rather than a hardcoded scene direction.
- [ ] The physical gauge displays `NEW MODEL SMELL REMAINING` as a comic interpretation of Release Age.
- [ ] The freshness presentation does not claim, rank, or imply model performance or quality.
- [ ] The Nose periodically inhales visible pixel scent particles while idle.
- [ ] It detects a nearby Inspector Cart or Model Vehicle and visibly tracks the vehicle within a bounded range.
- [ ] A close approach may trigger an exaggerated sneeze reaction without taking away steering or resetting the vehicle.
- [ ] The same Nose instance and behavior support both the opening and normal driving state.
- [ ] A browser test changes fixture dates, verifies the selected Dealership direction changes accordingly, and confirms a nearby vehicle triggers a visible reaction.
